Leukemia Overall Survival Prediction

Oct 2026
IPCW C-index 0.754 on an unseen hospital · clinical score IPSS-R 0.671

QRT × Institut Gustave Roussy challenge (Challenge Data, ENS Paris): predicting survival of myeloid leukemia patients from blood counts, karyotype text and gene mutations.

  • 91 engineered features, including a parser for free-text karyotypes; penalized Cox, random survival forest and XGBoost-Cox models.
  • Centre-grouped nested cross-validation: the ensemble scored 0.725 ± 0.021 and beat the best single model on 15 of 15 folds.
  • Kaplan–Meier and the IPCW C-index derived by hand and matched to library output; 33-page LaTeX write-up.
